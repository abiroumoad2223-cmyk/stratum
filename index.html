              About Stratum Skin

              Stratum started as a simple question: why does every skincare routine online look the same, when no two skin types react to the same products the same way? We're a small, independently run skincare guide based in Casablanca, Morocco. Instead of another list of "10 best products," we built a short quiz that reads your actual skin signals — barrier health, sensitivity, sun habits — and turns them into a routine that's specific to you, plus a plain-language reason for every product on the list.

              We're not a skincare brand and we don't sell anything ourselves. As an Amazon Associate, we link straight to real listings on Amazon and earn a small commission on qualifying purchases — at no extra cost to you. That's the whole business model: no hidden data resale, no spam.
