1. Information we collect

  • Quiz answers — your responses to the skin-type questionnaire, used to generate your routine.
  • Phone number — only if you voluntarily submit it through our SMS sign-up form, along with your explicit consent to receive texts.
  • Technical data — basic device/browser information collected automatically for security and analytics purposes.

We do not require an account and do not knowingly collect more personal information than needed to provide your routine and, where you opt in, to text you.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate and deliver your personalized skincare routine and PDF.
  • To send you SMS messages (new-routine tips, restock alerts, promotions) — only if you explicitly opted in via our sign-up form. See Section 4 below for details on SMS consent and how to opt out.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.

3. Third parties

Stratum participates in the Amazon Associates program. Product links on your results page go to Amazon; Amazon may collect information according to its own privacy policy once you leave our Site. As an Amazon Associate, Stratum earns from qualifying purchases.

To unlock certain content, the Site may display offers from a third-party offer network. Your quiz answers are not shared with that network; any information you choose to submit directly within an offer is governed by that partner's own privacy policy, which is shown before you submit anything to them.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. SMS / text messaging program

  • We only send you marketing texts if you affirmatively submit your phone number and check the consent box on our sign-up form.
  • Message types: new-routine alerts, restock notices, and promotional offers.
  • Message frequency: varies, up to approximately 4 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier and plan.
  • Consent to receive texts is never a condition of any purchase.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at stratumskin2627@gmail.com.
  • We do not share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & similar technologies

The Site may use basic cookies or local storage to remember your quiz progress and to measure Site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser, though parts of the Site may not work as intended.

6. Data retention

We keep quiz answers and phone numbers only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law, and delete them on request (see Section 7).

7. Your rights & choices

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your information.
  • Opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

To exercise any of these rights, email stratumskin2627@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
